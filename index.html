Welcome to Experiential Transformation

Drawing from the foundational work of J.L. Moreno and modern trauma-informed practices (inspired by Psychodrama New Jersey and the Phoenix Center in Pennsylvania), we use action methods to bring hidden stories into the open — through role reversal, doubling, mirroring, and spontaneous enactment.

Group Psychodrama

Experience the power of the group as protagonist, auxiliary egos, and audience — unlocking relational healing in real time.

Individual Sessions

One-on-one dramatic exploration — empty chair, role reversal, future projection — tailored to your unique narrative.

Training & Workshops

For clinicians: Build spontaneity and action-method skills in safe, experiential containers.

Why Psychodrama?

Unlike talk therapy alone, psychodrama brings the body, emotions, and relationships onto the "stage." It is especially powerful for trauma, grief, relational wounds, and identity exploration — creating corrective emotional experiences that words often cannot reach.
