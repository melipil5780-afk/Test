About the Work

Rooted in the legacy of J.L. Moreno, psychodrama invites the body, imagination, and relationships into the therapeutic space. Trained through NJ & PA psychodrama institutes, this work honors spontaneity, safety, and deep relational repair.

Therapy here is not about talking *about* your life — it is about gently stepping into it, together.

Services

Individual Psychodrama

One-on-one sessions using role reversal, empty chair, and future projection.
